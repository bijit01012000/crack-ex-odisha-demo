Part A

Q1. The Constitution deals with citizenship under:
  • A. Articles 1–4
  • B. Articles 5–11
  • C. Articles 12–35
  • D. Article 368 only
Answer: B
Q2. The provisions in Articles 5–11:
1. Provide permanent and exhaustive rules of citizenship.
2. Apply only at the commencement of the Constitution.
3. Empower Parliament to legislate further.
How many statements are correct?
  • A. Only one
  • B. Only two
  • C. All three
  • D. None
Answer: B
Q3. Under Article 5, a person domiciled in India at commencement becomes citizen if:
1. Born in India
2. Either parent born in India
3. Resident in India for at least 5 years immediately before commencement
Which are correct?
  • A. 1 and 2 only
  • B. 1, 2 and 3
  • C. 2 only
  • D. 3 only
Answer: B
Q4. Article 6 deals with citizenship of persons migrating from:
  • A. Bangladesh
  • B. Pakistan
  • C. Nepal
  • D. Sri Lanka
Answer: B
Q5. Under Article 6, persons migrating before July 19, 1948 required:
  • A. Registration only
  • B. Naturalisation
  • C. Residence of 6 months
  • D. No registration requirement
Answer: D
Q6. Article 7 relates to:
  • A. Persons migrating to Pakistan
  • B. Persons of Indian origin abroad
  • C. Illegal migrants
  • D. Foreign diplomats

  • A. Automatically became citizen

Q8. Article 8 deals with citizenship of:
  • A. Refugees

  • C. Illegal migrants
