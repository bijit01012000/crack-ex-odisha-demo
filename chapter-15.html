Q1
With reference to National Emergency under Article 352, consider the following statements:
1. It can be proclaimed even before actual war occurs.
2. It can be proclaimed on the ground of internal disturbance.
3. It requires written advice of the Cabinet.
How many of the above are correct?
  • A. Only one
  • B. Only two
  • C. All three
  • D. None
Answer B
Q2A proclamation of National Emergency must be approved by:
  • A. Simple majority of total membership of both Houses
  • B. Special majority of total membership of both Houses
  • C. Special majority of members present and voting in both Houses
  • D. Special majority: majority of total membership + 2/3rd of members present and voting
Answer D

  • A. Proclamation lapses immediately
  • B. Rajya Sabha approval alone is sufficient
  • C. Proclamation survives until 30 days from first sitting of reconstituted Lok Sabha
