Part A

Q1. The High Court serving Odisha sits at:
  • A. Bhubaneswar
  • B. Cuttack
  • C. Sambalpur
  • D. Rourkela
Answer: B
Why: Orissa High Court, Cuttack (est. 1948).
Q2. Odisha's Legislative Assembly was first constituted in (as a province):
  • A. 1936
  • B. 1946
  • C. 1951
  • D. 1961
Answer: A
Why: Separate province from 1 April 1936.
Q3. Odisha's legislature is:
  • A. bicameral
  • B. unicameral with only the Assembly
  • C. tr-cameral
  • D. directly elective
Answer: B
Why: No Legislative Council in Odisha.
Q4. Odisha's Rajya Sabha seats:
  • A. 7
  • B. 10
  • C. 12
  • D. 15
Answer: B
Why: Fourth Schedule allocation.
Q5. Number of Odisha districts commonly treated as PESA districts:
  • A. 5
  • B. 9
  • C. 10
  • D. 12
Answer: B
Why: Koraput, Rayagada, Mayurbhanj, Kandhamal, Malkangiri etc.
Q6. Odisha's Scheduled Areas governance is structured under:
  • A. Fifth Schedule
  • B. Sixth Schedule
  • C. Art 243
  • D. Art 356
Answer: A
Why: Tribes Advisory Council exists for the state.
Q7. Odisha panchayat elections are conducted by:
  • A. State Election Commission (Odisha)
  • B. Election Commission of India
  • C. Governor
  • D. District Collector
Answer: A
Why: Art 243K.
Q8. The classic-era Odisha Panchayat Samiti & Zila Parishad Act dates to:
  • A. 1948
  • B. 1956
  • C. 1961
  • D. 1964
Answer: C
Why: Orissa Panchayat Samitis and Zila Parishads Act, 1961.
Q9. Which community institution is consulted for land acquisition in Scheduled Areas of Odisha (as per PESA)?
  • A. Pal Sabha only
  • B. Gram Sabha under PESA
  • C. Zilla Parishad
  • D. TAC alone
Answer: B
Why: Gram Sabha-level consent customary for land diversion.
Q10. Odisha's religious-conversion governance statute is known as:
  • A. Freedom of Religion Act, 1967 (Odisha)
  • B. Conversion Reg 1950
  • C. Code of Conduct 2001
  • D. None
Answer: A
Why: Orissa Freedom of Religion Act 1967 – mirrored in Stainislaus (1977) SC judgment context.
Q11. Odisha-Chhattisgarh Mahanadi dispute went to:
  • A. Police tribunal
  • B. Mahanadi Water Disputes Tribunal under ISWD Act 1956 (constituted 2018)
  • C. Supreme Court only
  • D. River Boards Authority
Answer: B
Why: Under Art 262 machinery.
Q12. Odisha's Gram Nyayalayas rollout era:
  • A. 2010 onwards
  • B. 1998
  • C. 2004
  • D. 2016
Answer: A
Why: Gram Nyayalayas Act 2008; Odisha notified them from 2010.
Q13. Odisha Lokayukta Act was passed in:
  • A. 2014 (effective later)
  • B. 2006
  • C. 2019
  • D. 2011
Answer: A
Why: Odisha Lokayukta Act 2014; operationalised years later.
Q14. Odisha has which special Articles 371-type provision?
  • A. Full 371 sub-clause
  • B. None
  • C. 371J
  • D. 371B/Naga-type
Answer: B
Why: Odisha has no special Art 371 provision; tribal protection is 5th-Schedule based.
Q15. Odisha's '5T' governance reforms are driven by:
  • A. NITI Aayog
  • B. Odisha Chief Minister's Office
  • C. Finance Commission
  • D. Planning Commission
Answer: B
Why: 'Team Odisha, Technology, Transparency, Time and Transformation' initiative.
