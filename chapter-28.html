Landmark Constitutional Cases

Module Master Class Extras (Authored) · Master-class extras · 15 MCQs

Part A

Q1Kesavananda Bharati (1973) established:
  • A. the basic structure doctrine
  • B. Art 368 unlimited power
  • C. judicial review abolition
  • D. assessment of DPSP supremacy
Answer A
13-judge bench; Parliament cannot destroy core identity.
Q2S.R. Bommai (1994) primarily concerns:
  • A. Art 356, floor test, secularism
  • B. Right to Education
  • C. GST
  • D. Panchayati Raj
Answer A
Presidential satisfaction is judicially reviewable.
Q3Maneka Gandhi (1978) requires Art 21 procedure to be:
  • A. any procedure
  • B. just, fair and reasonable
  • C. approved by President
  • D. static
Answer B
Read substantive due process into Art 21.
Q4Puttaswamy (2017) declared privacy:
  • A. a legal right only
  • B. a fundamental right under Art 21
  • C. a DPSP
  • D. unjusticiable
Answer B
9-judge; proportionality discipline attached.
Q5Kihoto Hollohan (1992) concerned:
  • A. J&K
  • B. Tenth Schedule anti-defection judicial review
  • C. President's Rule
  • D. minority institutions
Answer B
Para 7 struck; limited review of Speaker's decisions allowed.
Q6NJAC (2015) was invalidated as:
  • A. aggravated Art 14 disorder
  • B. violation of judicial independence (basic structure)
  • C. breach of Art 19
  • D. federal encroachment
Answer B
4:1 majority; collegium revived by default.
Q7I.R. Coelho (2007) extended basic-structure review over:
  • A. Ninth Schedule laws added after 24-4-1973
  • B. ordinary bills
  • C. finance acts
  • D. elections
Answer A
Kesavananda-date cut-off; FRs and judicial review embedded.
Q8Indra Sawhney (1992) capped reservations at:
  • A. 50% with extraordinary-case exceptions
  • B. 60% absolute
  • C. 45% with no exception
  • D. none
Answer A
Creamy-layer exclusion for OBCs, carry-forward subject to ceiling.
Q9Shayara Bano (2017) declared unconstitutional:
  • A. triple talaq
  • B. polygamy per se
  • C. halal standards
  • D. uniform civil code
Answer A
3:2 majority — practice 'manifestly arbitrary'.
Q10Rajbala v Haryana (2015) upheld:
  • A. minimum educational qualification for panchayat polls
  • B. reservation in panchayats judicially
  • C. collegium
  • D. VP election rule
Answer A
3:2 majority — panchayat competence is wide for state legislature.
Q11Prem Shankar Shukla (1980) concerns:
  • A. handcuffing
  • B. solitary confinement
  • C. legal aid
  • D. speedy trial
Answer A
Art 21 bars routine handcuffing.
Q12ADM Jabalpur (1976) was expressly overruled in:
  • A. Puttaswamy
  • B. Kesavananda
  • C. Maneka
  • D. Bommai
Answer A
Puttaswamy overruled habeas corpus majority.
Q13Minerva Mills (1980) gave constitutional status to:
  • A. absolute FR primacy
  • B. balance between FRs and DPSPs as part of basic structure
  • C. Art 368 unlimited power
  • D. Ninth Schedule immunity
Answer B
Also curbed 42nd extension of Art 31C.
Q14Vishaka guidelines (1997) concerned:
  • A. sexual harassment at workplace
  • B. legal aid
  • C. environment
  • D. EWS quota
Answer A
Read into Arts 14/19/21; now POSH Act 2013.
Q15Lily Thomas (2013) struck down:
  • A. Sec 8(4) RP Act reprieve for convicted legislators
  • B. Sec 499 IPC
  • C. Art 365
  • D. Art 356
Answer A
Immediate disqualification on conviction.
