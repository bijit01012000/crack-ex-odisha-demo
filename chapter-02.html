Reason: Seats were divided among General, Muslim and Sikh communities in provinces.
15.
Assertion: The Constituent Assembly was initially not a sovereign body.
Reason: It was created under the authority of the British Cabinet Mission Plan.
16.
Assertion: Members from princely states were elected by proportional representation.
Reason: Provincial members were elected through single transferable vote.
17.
Assertion: The Constituent Assembly functioned both as a constitution-making body and a legislative body.
Reason: After independence, it exercised full legislative powers.
18.
Assertion: The Cripps Proposals failed because they guaranteed immediate independence.
Reason: They proposed dominion status after the war.
19.
Assertion: The Cabinet Mission Plan rejected the idea of Pakistan outright.
Reason: It proposed grouping of provinces into sections.
20.
Assertion: The principle of proportional representation ensured minority safeguards in the Constituent
Assembly.
Reason: Members were elected through single transferable vote.
