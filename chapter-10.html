Part A

Q1.
With reference to Article 368, consider the following statements:
1. The power of Parliament to amend the Constitution flows from Article 245.
2. The amendment power under Article 368 is a constituent power and not an ordinary legislative
power.
3. A Constitutional Amendment Bill requires prior recommendation of the President.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  • A. 2 only
  • B. 1 and 3 only
  • C. 2 and 3 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: A
Q2. Consider the following statements regarding Constitutional Amendment Bills:
1. They can be introduced in either House of Parliament.
2. They may be introduced by a private member.
3. They are subject to joint sitting in case of deadlock.
Which of the above statements is/are correct?
  • A. 1 and 2 only
  • B. 2 and 3 only
  • C. 1 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: A
Q3. Which of the following correctly describes “special majority” under Article 368?
  • A. Majority of total membership of the House only
  • B. Two-thirds of members present and voting only
  • C. Majority of total membership and two-thirds of members present and voting
  • D. Two-thirds of total membership of the House
Answer: C
Q4. Consider the following provisions:
1. Election of the President
2. Supreme Court and High Courts
3. Distribution of legislative powers between Union and States
4. Official language provisions
Which of the above require ratification by at least half of the State Legislatures?
  • A. 1, 2 and 3 only
  • B. 1, 3 and 4 only
  • C. 2 and 3 only
  • D. 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: D
Q5. Which of the following can be amended by a simple majority of Parliament (outside Article 368)?
1. Creation of new States
2. Citizenship provisions
3. Delimitation of constituencies
4. Salaries of judges of Supreme Court
  • A. 1 and 3 only
  • B. 1, 2 and 3 only
  • C. 2 and 4 only
  • D. 1, 2, 3 and 4
Answer: C

  • C. 3 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3

Q7. If Parliament passes a Constitutional Amendment Bill and the President withholds assent, which of the

Q9. Which of the following was inserted by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment regarding amendment power?

  • D. Provided veto power to States

  • B. Struck down parts of 42nd Amendment expanding Article 31C
