Part A

Q1. The Election Commission of India is:
  • A. a constitutional body
  • B. a statutory body
  • C. an executive resolution body
  • D. an advisory body only
Answer: A
Why: Art 324.
Q2. Article 280 provides for:
  • A. Finance Commission
  • B. GST Council
  • C. Election Commission
  • D. UPSC
Answer: A
Why: Constituted every 5 years for devolution, grants-in-aid, grants principles.
Q3. The Comptroller and Auditor-General audits:
  • A. Union and state accounts
  • B. public sector enterprises
  • C. bodies substantially financed by government
  • D. all of these
Answer: D
Why: Art 148 + DPC Act 1971.
Q4. The Attorney-General of India holds office during:
  • A. the President's pleasure
  • B. a fixed 5-year term
  • C. Parliament's pleasure
  • D. CJI appointment
Answer: A
Why: Art 76.
Q5. NHRC, CIC, NGT and Lokpal are respectively:
  • A. constitutional bodies
  • B. statutory bodies
  • C. extra-constitutional advisory bodies
  • D. judicial bodies
Answer: B
Why: Created by statutes (1993, 2005, 2010, 2013).
Q6. Article 315 establishes:
  • A. CAG
  • B. Union and State Public Service Commissions
  • C. Finance Commission
  • D. Election Commission
Answer: B
Why: Art 315-323: UPSC/SPSC.
Q7. The first chairman of the Finance Commission was:
  • A. K.C. Neogy
  • B. K. Santhanam
  • C. P.N. Thiru
  • D. Morarji Desai
Answer: A
Why: FC-1 (1951).
Q8. NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in:
  • A. 2012
  • B. 2013
  • C. 2014
  • D. 2015
Answer: D
Why: January 1, 2015.
Q9. GST Council decisions require:
  • A. simple majority
  • B. 3/4 of weighted votes (Centre 1/3, states 2/3)
  • C. unanimity
  • D. 2/3 of members
Answer: B
Why: Art 279A(9).
Q10. The National Commission for Backward Classes became a constitutional body under:
  • A. 102nd Amendment
  • B. 103rd Amendment
  • C. 89th Amendment
  • D. 97th Amendment
Answer: A
Why: Art 338B (2018).
Q11. The first Administrative Reforms Commission (1966) was chaired by:
  • A. Morarji Desai
  • B. H.M. Patel
  • C. K. Hanumanthaiah
  • D. Gadgil
Answer: A
Why: 1966-70.
Q12. Speaker's disqualification decisions under the Tenth Schedule face:
  • A. no judicial review
  • B. limited judicial review (procedural + mala fide)
  • C. full appellate review
  • D. President override
Answer: B
Why: Kihoto Hollohan (1992).
Q13. Post-2019 amendment, the NHRC chairperson must be:
  • A. any retired Supreme Court judge
  • B. a former Chief Justice of India
  • C. an advocate of 20 years
  • D. a Governor
Answer: B
Why: NHRC (Amendment) Act 2019.
Q14. Which of the following is NOT a constitutional body?
  • A. Election Commission
  • B. Finance Commission
  • C. NITI Aayog
  • D. National Commission for STs
Answer: C
Why: NITI Aayog is executive-resolved; rest have constitutional roots.
Q15. Public Accounts Committee primarily examines:
  • A. budget estimates
  • B. appropriation accounts and CAG reports
  • C. demands for grants
  • D. Finance Bill
Answer: B
Why: Post-expenditure audit accountability.
