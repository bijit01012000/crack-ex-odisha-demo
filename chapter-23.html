Part A

Q1. With reference to the Governor, consider:
1. The Constitution envisages the same parliamentary system in states as at the Centre.
2. Articles 153 to 167 deal with the State Executive.
3. There is a provision for a Vice-Governor in states similar to the Vice-President at the Centre.
  • A. 1 and 2 only
  • B. 2 only
  • C. 1 and 3 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: B
Q2. The Governor is:
  • A. Directly elected by the people of the state
  • B. Indirectly elected by the state legislature
  • C. Appointed by the President by warrant under his hand and seal
  • D. Nominated by the Chief Minister
Answer: C
Q3. Which of the following statements are correct regarding the Governor’s role?
1. He is the nominal executive head of the state.
2. He acts as an agent of the Central Government.
3. He is subordinate to the Union Council of Ministers.
  • A. 1 only
  • B. 1 and 2 only
  • C. 2 and 3 only
  • D. 1, 2 and 3
Answer: D
Q4. The 7th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1956 enabled:
  • A. Direct election of Governors
  • B. Removal of the Governor by Parliament
  • C. Appointment of the same person as Governor for two or more states
  • D. Abolition of the office of Governor
Answer: C
Q5. The Draft Constitution originally provided for:
  • A. Indirect election of Governor
