State Government & Legislature

Q1The Chief Minister is appointed by:
  • A. the Governor
  • B. the President
  • C. the state legislature
  • D. the Chief Justice of the High Court
Answer A
Art 164: Governor appoints CM (on convention: leader of majority).
Q2A non-member appointed Chief Minister must become a member of the legislature within:
  • A. one month
  • B. six months
  • C. one year
  • D. three months
Answer B
Art 164(4).
Q3The state Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to:
  • A. the Governor
  • B. the Legislative Assembly
  • C. both Houses
  • D. the High Court
Answer B
Art 164(2).
Q4The Chief Minister's resignation automatically dissolves:
  • A. the Council of Ministers
  • B. the Legislative Assembly
  • C. the Legislative Council
  • D. Neither
Answer A
CM's departure dissolves his council; assembly continues.
Q5The size of the state Council of Ministers is capped at:
  • A. 10% of assembly
  • B. 15% of assembly
  • C. 20% of assembly
  • D. 25% of assembly
Answer B
Art 164(1A); minimum 12.
Q6The Chief Minister really holds office so long as he enjoys:
  • A. the Governor's pleasure
  • B. the President's pleasure
  • C. majority in the Assembly
  • D. no fixed tenure
Answer C
Constitutional convention over Art 164(1).
Q7Who summons and prorogues the State Legislature?
  • A. Speaker
  • B. Chief Minister
  • C. Governor
  • D. Chairman of Council
Answer C
Art 174.
Q8A Legislative Council is created or abolished by:
  • A. special majority under Art 368
  • B. a law of Parliament under Art 169 after assembly resolution
  • C. gubernatorial order
  • D. ordinance
Answer B
Art 169.
Q9How many states currently have Legislative Councils?
  • A. 5
  • B. 6
  • C. 7
  • D. 8
Answer B
AP, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, UP (6).
Q10The maximum strength of a Legislative Council is:
  • A. one-third of the assembly
  • B. one-fourth of the assembly
  • C. one-half of assembly
  • D. 25 members
Answer A
Art 171; minimum 40.
Q11The Legislative Council can delay an Assembly-passed ordinary bill for a maximum of:
  • A. 14 days
  • B. 3 months first, 1 month later
  • C. 6 months
  • D. no limit
Answer C
Art 197 mechanism.
Q12The executive power of a state vests in:
  • A. the Chief Minister
  • B. the Governor
  • C. the Legislative Assembly
  • D. the High Court
Answer B
Art 154; exercised on CoM aid and advice.
Q13Under Art 356 President's Rule in a state, the High Court's jurisdiction is:
  • A. suspended
  • B. never suspended
  • C. transferred to SC
  • D. shared with Governor
Answer B
Art 357 scope excludes courts.
Q14Law officer of a state government is:
  • A. Advocate General
  • B. Attorney General
  • C. Solicitor General
  • D. Public Prosecutor
Answer A
Art 165.
Q15Fifth Schedule-related special responsibility of Senior Governorship concerns:
  • A. NE state security
  • B. administration of Scheduled Areas
  • C. anti-defection
  • D. finance
Answer B
Governor reports to President on Scheduled Areas; chairs/directs TAC machinery.
