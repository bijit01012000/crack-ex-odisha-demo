  • B. British parliamentary sovereignty

Q96. The Indian Independence Act, 1947 can best be described as:
  • A. Grant of responsible government
  • B. Transfer of sovereignty
  • C. Introduction of dyarchy
  • D. Establishment of federalism
Answer: B
Q97. The Constituent Assembly derived its authority after 15 August 1947 from:
  • A. British Parliament
  • B. Cabinet Mission Plan
  • C. Indian Independence Act
  • D. Government of India Act 1935
Answer: C
Q98. Under the 1947 Act, laws made by dominion legislatures could be invalidated by:
